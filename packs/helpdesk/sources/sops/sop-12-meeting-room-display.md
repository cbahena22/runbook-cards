# SOP: Meeting room display shows no picture
related: sop-06-printer-queue

Purpose: meeting room display shows no picture.

Steps
1) Check the display is on the right input.
Step 2: Check the cable is firmly seated at both ends.
3. Restart the room control unit.
4) Swap the cable with a spare.
Step 5: Log the room and the fault in the facilities sheet.

Check: Picture shows; the fault is logged.
Escalate to: Facilities

Old name: 'AV room'.

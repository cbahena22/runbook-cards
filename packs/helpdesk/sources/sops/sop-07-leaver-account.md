# SOP: Close the account of someone who has left
related: sop-05-shared-mailbox

Purpose: close the account of someone who has left.

Steps
1) Wait for the HR leaver notice, never act on a verbal request.
Step 2: Disable the user in the directory console.
3. Reset the password to a random value.
4) Remove the user from all groups.
Step 5: Convert the mailbox to a shared mailbox for the manager.
6. Collect the laptop and badge.
7) Wipe the laptop after 30 days.

Check: HR notice attached; account shows disabled; laptop recorded as returned.
Escalate to: HR partner

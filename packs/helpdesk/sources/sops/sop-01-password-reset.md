# SOP: Password reset for a locked-out user
related: sop-04-mfa-device-lost

Purpose: password reset for a locked-out user.

Steps
1) Confirm the caller's identity with two questions from the HR record.
Step 2: Open the directory console and find the account.
3. Click Reset password and tick 'must change at next logon'.
4) Read the temporary password to the caller by phone only.
Step 5: Ask them to sign in and choose a new password.
6. Log the ticket as resolved.

Check: Identity checked and logged; the temporary password was never sent by chat or mail.
Escalate to: Service desk lead
